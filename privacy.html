GigTax Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026
Developer: PeakNova

GigTax ("the app") is a tax organizer for US gig workers and freelancers: a mileage logbook, receipt capture, deduction tracking, and tax estimates. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Location. Only when you enable trip tracking (manual or automatic). Location is used solely to measure trip distances for your mileage log. Automatic trip detection uses background location only while the feature is switched on, and detected trips are kept for your review — nothing is uploaded or shared without your action.
  • Receipt photos. Stored on your device only, so you have proof of purchase for deductions. They are never uploaded unless you sign in and enable cloud backup.
  • Account information. The app works without an account. If you optionally link a Google account, we receive your basic profile (name, email) to provide encrypted cloud backup via Google Firebase/Firestore.
  • Tax entries you type. Trips, income, expenses, and notes you enter are stored on your device, and in your private Firebase backup only if you sign in.

How we use data

  • To compute your mileage log, deduction totals, and tax estimates on your device.
  • To back up and restore your data across your devices when you sign in (Google Firebase).
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with:

  • Google Firebase / Firestore — only if you sign in, to store your encrypted backup. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play billing — if you purchase Premium, the purchase is processed by Google Play under its own terms.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app. If you signed in, you can request deletion of your cloud backup by emailing us; we will delete it within 30 days.

Children's privacy

GigTax is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
